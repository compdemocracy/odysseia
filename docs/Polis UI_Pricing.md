**Individual**  
**For personal, independent use**  
For individuals exploring Polis for personal projects, independent research, learning, or experimentation.

* Non-commercial use only  
* Not affiliated with an organization, campaign, or employer  
* No client work or organizational deployment  
* If you later use Polis as part of an organization, campaign, or paid work, you can upgrade at any time.

## **Nonprofit / Public-interest**

**For mission-driven organizations**

For registered nonprofits, academic researchers, and public-interest projects using Polis to support civic participation, research, or community dialogue.

* Non-commercial, mission-aligned use  
* For organizations operating in the public interest  
* Not for political campaigns or candidates

This tier is offered at a reduced rate to support democratic and civic work.

---

## **Commercial / Full price**

**For organizations, campaigns, and professional use**

For companies, consultants, and political campaigns using Polis in professional, organizational, or revenue-generating contexts.

* Includes for-profit organizations and political campaigns  
* Same product and features as all other tiers  
* No discounts, subsidies, or pro bono access

This tier reflects the full operational cost of running Polis Pro.
